    Spatial AILive

    Food Bank Spatial Twin

    Digital twins usually start from CAD drawings and laser scans, which puts them out of reach for most non-profits. Is a public video tour enough to start one?

    Frames of a public news tour of a food bank become Gaussian-splat captures through a world model, a scripted Blender model fills in the parts the video never showed, and OpenUSD ties both to operations data served from a serverless AWS backend. The twin runs in the browser and in NVIDIA Omniverse on EC2 GPUs. Built in seven days for about $1.54 of compute, with a reference architecture mapped to AWS, Azure and Google Cloud and real sensors priced at about $12 a month to run.

    • World model API
    • 3D Gaussian splatting
    • Blender
    • OpenUSD
    • AWS SAM + Lambda
    • Omniverse on EC2
    • three.js

    Digital twinLive

    Michelin Kitchen Twin

    Factory planning ideas like MRP, routings and OEE are hard to show with plant data nobody can share. A famous kitchen makes them visible to anyone.

    A 3D twin of a three-star kitchen running a full tasting-menu service. Covers are the build plan, 53 recipes are bills of materials and routings, and a discrete-event simulation plays out tickets, stations and bottlenecks minute by minute. Parallel coding agents built the product, data, simulation and 3D layers.

    • three.js
    • Discrete-event simulation
    • MRP and BOMs
    • OpenUSD export
    • Coding agents

    Baby Senses Lab

    AI world models train on adult-quality data from day one. Infants start with blurry, muffled input and still learn fast. What does that staging buy a model?

    One world model per sense (vision, hearing, touch, body position), each fed through an age-tuned filter and grown as it trains, then fused. The public site lets parents see and hear like a baby of any age; researchers get the findings, papers and code.

    • PyTorch
    • World models
    • Network growth
    • Multisensory fusion

    On-device AIv1.1 released

    LabTrends

    A family's lab results sit in PDFs and paper scans from different clinics, and seeing a trend usually means handing health data to someone's cloud.

    An Android app that reads reports with on-device OCR, parses biomarkers with rules backed by a small local language model, and has a person confirm every value before it is saved. The release build has no internet permission at all.

    • React Native + Expo
    • Local LLM (llama.rn)
    • On-device OCR
    • Encrypted SQLite backups

    Academic Matchmaker

    Prospective PhD students choose advisors from rankings and cold emails rather than research fit.

    Drop in a CV or paper and a small language model running in the browser on WebGPU extracts research interests; embeddings then rank faculty across the top 100 universities. Nothing leaves the device. A scheduled pipeline is rebuilding the faculty index from open scholarly data.

    • Transformers.js
    • WebGPU
    • Small LLM (ONNX)
    • Embeddings search
